Fault-Tolerant Quadruped Locomotion

Reinforcement Learning Policies for Robot Control

Project Outline

This project aims at learning a control policy capable of rapidly adapting to sudden joint failures in a medium-size quadruped robot locomoting on uneven terrain. To this end, we adopt a deep Reinforcement Learning framework based on an asymmetric actor-critic architecture, trained using Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). Leveraging asymmetric training, we augment the learning objective with an auxiliary latent alignment term that encourages the actor to reconstruct fault-related privileged representations from a history of proprioceptive observations, while the critic has access to explicit fault information. The resulting policy operates at 50 Hz and outputs joint position targets tracked by a low-level PD controller running at 1 kHz.

The following MuJoCo simulations show the Kyon quadruped robot, either equipped or not with its upper limbs, tracking velocity commands while powered by our control policies.
